> **TL;DR.** Europol's widely-cited "90% of online content AI-generated by 2026" has not come true at the full-web level, but platform-by-platform the picture is uneven and rising fast. TikTok labeled 1.3B+ AI videos; LinkedIn is the most AI-text-saturated platform (OriginalityAI studies); X has the most human text per unit of volume; YouTube Shorts is flooded, long-form is not. Detection accuracy remains unreliable.

## By format

### Text

- **LinkedIn is the worst offender.** OriginalityAI's 2025 dataset re-run in 2026 showed ~60% of top-engaging LinkedIn posts scoring as "likely AI." Engagement is higher for AI-assisted than pure-AI and pure-human, creating an optimizer for mid-quality AI output.
- **Substack retains a craft floor.** Paid newsletters especially; pure-AI Substacks have visible churn patterns.
- **X/Twitter**: Grok auto-posts + shitposting farms inflated AI share, but the platform's reward model (replies, retweets) still favors human voice.

### Image

- **Flux and Midjourney** are the dominant gens (see flux became the builders choice midjourney stays the aesthete).
- **Nano Banana** hit Google's "cultural moment" in image — high meme share on IG and X.
- Image detection accuracy is now in the 70–85% range for frontier gens; down from 95%+ on older models.

### Video

- **YouTube Shorts** became the AI-video dumping ground — listicle / faceless / kids-content farms dominate. TikTok cracked down earlier; Shorts algorithm is more forgiving.
- **Long-form YouTube** holds — MrBeast-tier production still beats any AI gen, and viewers reward human presence.
- **Sora 2 and Veo 3** shifted the quality floor dramatically in 2026. "Can't tell" is now the norm for short clips.

### Voice

- **ElevenLabs-cloned voices** ubiquitous in AI podcasts + faceless YT.
- Deepfake scam voice is a growing problem on Facebook/YT/TikTok/IG (per investigations 2026-04).
- NewsGuard flagged 3,006+ AI content-farm sites in 16 languages.

## The labeling-policy landscape

| Platform | Policy (Apr 2026) | Reality |

| **TikTok** | Mandatory disclosure for synthetic realistic media; uses C2PA Content Credentials auto-detection; tested "reduce AI in feed" toggle | Decent enforcement; 1.3B+ labeled |

## Detection tool accuracy (2026)

- **OriginalityAI**: best-in-class for GPT-class text; accuracy drops on Claude and polished-by-human flows.
- **GPTZero**: widely used in education, high false-positive on non-native English writers.
- **Pangram** (NewsGuard partner): strong on content-farm identification.
- **Image/video**: C2PA signatures help when present, but stripping is trivial.

**Honest take:** no consumer-grade detector is reliable enough to be actionable at the individual post level. They work at population level (content-farm detection) only.

## Editorial take (Rik)

Two realities:

1. **AI is winning the mid.** Mid-quality text, mid-quality video, faceless channels, LinkedIn thought-leader posts — AI dominates and will keep dominating. The algorithm rewards volume and there's no defensive moat here.
2. **The craft ceiling is rising.** See ai slop vs ai craft the 2026 creators line. Long-form YouTube, paid Substacks, personal brands with voice — these are *more* valuable now, not less, because the floor is flooded with slop.

Strategic implication for a creator in 2026: don't fight the flood. Either accept you're on the AI-assisted optimizer treadmill (and scale accordingly), or plant a flag above it. Middle positions get crushed.
