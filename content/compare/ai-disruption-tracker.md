## The pattern

1. **Design tools**: AI gen is now good enough for 80% of use cases Figma/Adobe historically owned. Professional workflows still safe for ~2-3 yrs.
2. **Horizontal SaaS**: the "seat" is the pricing unit being destroyed. Agents don't need seats. Everyone pivoting to "outcome pricing" — nobody's got it right yet.
3. **Developer infrastructure**: the most complete substitution — Stack Overflow is the canary. 78% traffic decline and they still have a business because the data became training substrate.

## Editorial takeaway

The "SaaSpocalypse" framing is half right. It's not that SaaS dies — it's that **the seat-based pricing model dies**, and the winners are whoever repackages fast (Anthropic/OpenAI on the AI side; MCP-first pure-plays on the app side).

Short-term opportunity for builders: every disrupted category has a "vertical AI-native" replacement that Rik could ship. That's the actual playbook under levelsio is the proof of concept for one person vibe businesses — find a SaaS category where the seat model is rotting, ship the 10x cheaper agent version.
